Privacy Policy

What we collect. If you create an account: your name, email address and a salted, hashed password. When you generate notes: the topic text you enter, your language/level choices and the generated notes. We also keep basic usage and error logs (time, action, anonymous IDs) to protect the service and enforce daily limits.

Where it is stored. In Google Sheets and Google Drive owned by the academy administrator. Your private notes are not made public. Notes become readable by others only if you (or the administrator) publish them.

AI processing. The topic you enter is sent to a third-party AI provider (for example Google Gemini) to generate your notes. Do not enter confidential or sensitive personal information.

Your choices. You can rename or delete your notes at any time from My Library. To delete your account, contact us.

On your device. The app stores your sign-in session, theme and reading preferences in your browser's local storage.
